Step-by-Step Adoption Roadmap

01
Eligibility Check

Applicants must meet legal age standards, demonstrate financial stability and pass clean background verification.

02
Documentation

Submit National ID/Passport, income certificates, medical records and police clearance reports through our secure portal.

03
Home Assessment

Authorized social officers conduct home visits to assess living conditions, safety and family readiness.

04
Matching Process

Our committee carefully matches applicants with children based on needs, background and emotional compatibility.

05
Legal Guardianship

Formal court proceedings and legal documentation are finalized in accordance with national child custody laws.

06
Post-Adoption Follow-up

Periodic check-ins and support counseling are provided during the first 12 months to ensure smooth integration.

Applicant Requirements

✓
Age Limit: Primary applicants must be between 25 and 55 years old.
✓
Marital Status: Married couples (minimum 2 years of marriage) or eligible single applicants.
✓
Financial Capability: Stable monthly income to ensure comfortable living, healthcare, and education.
✓
Health Status: Physically and mentally capable of caring for a child (verified by medical certificate).
✓
Background Check: Clean legal record with no criminal history.
